Akhil Bharat Vivekananda Yuva Mahamandal

BE AND MAKE

I am born to organise these young men; nay, hundreds more in every city are ready to join me; and I want to send them rolling like irresistible waves over India, bringing comfort, morality, religion, education to the doors of the meanest and the most downtrodden. And this I will do or die. – Swami Vivekananda

The Idea

Man is the basic thing, and man-making is the fundamental work. Everything, from personal life to society, depends on the quality of the people concerned, which is ruled by their character; and character can be built through practice. So we need not lose hope: we can practise, and improve our lives. An individual may, of course, attempt man-making in himself or in others; but to make the attempt everywhere, and not leave it to the casual efforts of a few, an organized effort covering the whole country is needed. That is the idea behind the Mahamandal.

Overview

The Mahamandal began its journey in 1967, to spread Swami Vivekananda’s idea of man-making and character-building among the youth, as an organized youth movement for the good of society from the personal to the national level. To make the idea of character-building more welcome to the young, the Mahamandal follows several methods.

Youth Training Programmes

The Mahamandal holds training camps for the youth, from the national to the regional level, to explain Swami Vivekananda’s man-making and character-building ideas and to encourage the young to practise them in daily life.

Study Circle

Camps are usually held once a year, and only a few can join them. But to build character one must receive good ideas regularly, and this is done through the weekly study circle held by every centre.

Books

The ideas and methods of the Mahamandal are available in about a hundred books, booklets, and audio recordings, in English, Hindi, Bengali, Odia, and Telugu.

Bulletins

Vivek-Jivan, the Mahamandal’s bilingual monthly (in English and Bengali), has been brought out every month since 1968. Vivek-Anjan, a quarterly, is published in Hindi.

Social Services

To worship the divinity within man, the centres run various social services – charitable dispensaries, free coaching classes, adult education centres, schools for children, students’ homes, feeding programmes, libraries, and the like.

Adolescent Development (Kishor Vikash Vahini)

Adolescence is the most important stage of human life, and much of the future depends on it. For teenagers the Mahamandal has a separate method of training, developed with the collective help of several schoolteachers and senior workers of the Mahamandal.

Children’s Wing (Vivek Vahini)

Vivek Vahini, the children’s wing of the Mahamandal, is run by many of its units to plant life-giving ideas in children. The Mahamandal believes that the right growth of body, mind, and heart must begin at a very tender age, and that this needs a programme specially suited to children; the scheme of Vivek Vahini is a full approach to that end.

Centres

The work of the Mahamandal is carried on in about three hundred centres – the actual number varies – from which one may receive the life-giving ideas of Swami Vivekananda and join hands with this noble movement.

Sister Organization

Sarada Nari Sangathan, a sister organization of the Mahamandal founded in 1989, works independently on the same lines. It now has about seventy-five centres, each holding a weekly study circle, and it organizes All-India, zonal, and local training camps for women, where the practical methods of character-building, mental concentration, physical exercise, and the like are taught. Hundreds of women attend these camps through the year.

Special Features

The Mahamandal reaches youth where they are Speaks in a language they can understand Does not ask them to leave their home and occupation Helps them in understanding life and its purpose, in setting a goal Provides them with a complete programme to achieve the goal Trains them in simple and scientific methods that can be applied in everyday life Not only individually but as a collective To build a nation-wide youth movement to make enlightened citizens in an organized way.

ABVYM Unit Guide

A free Android application carrying the working knowledge of the Mahamandal to its units and organizers, in English, Hindi and Bengali. It presents to every sincere worker the ideas, methods, and procedures for organizing the work, mostly in the form of questions and short answers. It works offline, holds no accounts, and shows no advertising.
